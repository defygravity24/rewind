Rewind — Privacy Policy

Effective August 19, 2026

The short version

Rewind works entirely on your device. There is no account, no server, and nothing is ever uploaded. We never sell data, show ads, or track you.

What the app stores

Your daily entries (ratings, answers to the prompts, the shows, food, drinks, people and places you tag) and any photos or videos you attach. All of it is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. Nothing is sent anywhere. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Your photos & videos

Photos and videos you add are copied into the app's private storage using the standard iOS photo picker. Rewind never accesses your photo library beyond the items you explicitly choose, and never uploads them. Photos are resized on your device to save space. Media is not included in JSON exports — it stays on the device you added it to.

Backups & export

Your data is included in your normal iPhone backup, managed by Apple under your own Apple account. You can export everything you've written as a JSON file at any time from inside the app, and import it on another device.

Deleting your data

Delete entries any time in the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Rewind — Support

Rewind the Day is a prompt-based daily journal: rate the day, answer a few prompts, tag the shows, food, drinks, people and places, and add photos.

Frequently asked

Where is my data? On your device only. Nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy.

Can I move it to a new phone? Yes — Export JSON in the app, then Import JSON on the new device. Your iPhone backup also includes it.

Can I use it on the web? Yes — defygravity24.github.io/rewind runs the same app in any browser (data is separate per device).
